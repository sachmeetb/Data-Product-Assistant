Risk Data Architecture · Version 1.0 · 16 November 2026

BCBS 239 Risk Data Model — Fully Attributed

An enterprise logical and physical data model for risk data aggregation and risk reporting, aligned to the Basel Committee's 14 Principles (BCBS 239). Every attribute carries a definition, type, key, nullability, critical-data-element status, data-quality dimensions and rule, ownership, authoritative source, lineage and principle traceability.

Model is a reference design: owners, systems and example values are illustrative and should be replaced with the institution's own. BCBS 239 principle summaries are paraphrased from BCBS, "Principles for effective risk data aggregation and risk reporting" (January 2013).
